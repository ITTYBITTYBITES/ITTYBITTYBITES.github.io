A new book by Dr. Earle S. Gregory

The Reality of Death:
Are You Ready?

A practical and spiritual guide to facing life's final chapter with peace, purpose, and unshakable hope in Christ. By Dr. Earle S. Gregory.

Get the book → Free guide

By Dr. Earle S. Gregory · Published by Redemption Press, 2026

What if preparing for death could bring peace, purpose, and a renewed joy for living? In The Reality of Death: Are You Ready?, readers are guided through both the practical and spiritual decisions that shape eternity — living fully, leaving wisely, and facing the end with unshakable hope in Christ.

A glimpse inside

The word death caught your attention when you read the title online or in a bookstore. It could have elicited various responses, memories, or fears for you. I hope that by the time you finish reading this book, you will have several insights into the end of life, including those of acceptance and anticipation.

Like many people in their seventies, I have encountered death on many occasions. Some have affected me personally. In addition, I have helped and supported others through the deaths of children, teenagers, parents, graduate students, and coworkers. Each has shown me how poorly prepared many in Western society are for the finality of this life.

I pray that my personal experience, along with research, knowledge of the Word, the Holy Spirit’s inspiration, and your own journey toward becoming more like Jesus Christ will help you find peace with death. As the psalmist says, our life is but a wisp of air.

What readers will find

  • Biblical truth — Scriptural perspective on death, eternity, readiness, and the hope found in Christ.
  • Practical wisdom — Guidance for thinking through real-world decisions before they become urgent.
  • Peace and confidence — Encouragement to strengthen your relationship with God and face the future with assurance.
  • Purpose today — A reminder that preparing for what comes next can reshape how you live right now.

By reading this book, you will learn that:

  • Fear of death can be replaced with peace.
  • Uncertainty about eternity can become confident hope.
  • Putting things off only increases the burden.
  • A “good life” is not the same as a prepared life.
  • Regret can be replaced with reconciliation.
  • A passive faith can become an active, purposeful walk with God.

Questions & answers

Who is this book for?+

People who have lived a full life, built a career and raised a family, and carry quiet questions about what comes next — anyone who senses time is moving faster and wants peace, clarity, and confidence about eternity.

What will I learn from this book?+

You will learn that fear of death can be replaced with peace; uncertainty about eternity can become confident hope; putting things off only increases the burden; a 'good life' is not the same as a prepared life; regret can be replaced with reconciliation; a passive faith can become an active walk with God; and you can live fully now when you are no longer afraid of what comes next.
