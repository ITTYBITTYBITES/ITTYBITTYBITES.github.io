The Author

Dr. Earle S. Gregory

Dr. Earle S. Gregory is a seasoned educator, author, and ministry leader who came to faith in Christ at age sixty-seven. Now seventy-six, he draws on decades of professional experience, ministry involvement, and biblical study to encourage others to live intentionally, prepare wisely, and face eternity with confidence in Christ.

His writing comes from a life spent in classrooms and churches — and from the practical work of serving as an executor of wills and power of attorney for families facing loss. That experience shaped The Reality of Death: Are You Ready?, which pairs the practical (wills, finances, end-of-life wishes) with the spiritual (reconciliation, assurance, and peace with God).
