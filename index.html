Mission statement

ALIGNED WITH HEAVEN INC was created to bring the God of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and His Son, Jesus, Yeshua into the passions of people, both lost and found.

Vision statement

ALIGNED WITH HEAVEN INC wants to be the provider of tools for the greatest harvest of souls ever known to man.

About us

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization focused on helping make the world a better, happier place — introducing people to Jesus, Father God and The Holy Spirit, and supplying weapons for spiritual warfare to churches, Christian groups, colleges, schools and universities, and anyone who wants to fight the good fight.

Getting God involved in our passions

The first passion we are focusing on is Golf. What better way to involve God in your game than having Him join you in your round of golf with Golfing With The King, a hole by hole "Spiritual Caddy" to take along with you.
