What to Think About the Results of the 2026 Elections?

Many have weighed in on the results of the 2026 elections. Senator Ted Cruz (Texas) called it “a Watergate-level repudiation” as Democrat James Talarico was elected to the Senate and statewide offices in Texas turned blue. But what were voters throughout the country repudiating? Was it the price of gasoline and groceries? Was it the Iran War? Was it restrictions on those who enter the country illegally? Was it President Trump?

I believe the reason goes far deeper than any of these. Many in this country are blinded by Satan. They cannot see the forest through the trees. Many lack a biblical perspective. Instead, they hold a humanistic perspective, grounded in this life, with no concern for an afterlife. Such an individual focuses on science, critical thinking, and empathy, without the supernatural.

Rabbi Jonathan Cahn, Apostle Dutch Sheets, and Apostle Chuck Pierce shared insights you may find helpful if you want to make sense of what occurred:

  • Rabbi Johnathan Cahn, “The Warning, the Backlash, and the Window.” https://www.hopeoftheworld.org/Sapphires/index.php?op=single&id=5050
  • Apostle Dutch Sheets, “My Thoughts Regarding the Midterm Elections.” https://www.givehim15.com/post/november-5-2026
  • Apostle Chuck Pierce, “A Key Word from 2019 for Texas and America.” https://mycharisma.com/propheticrevival/chuck-pierce-the-2019-prophecy-unfolding-as-texas-election-shakes-america/

I would encourage those who may be concerned about what occurred this past Tuesday to remember 2 Chronicles 7:14: “If my people who are called by My name will humble themselves, and pray and seek My face, and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin and heal their land.”

Simply, the hope for the United States of America is not in one person, lower prices, more or less freedom, lower or higher taxes, changes in government, socialism, or anything related to human beings. This country’s hope lies in the Christian principles on which it was founded.

Let us pray to the triune God, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, for a Third Great Awakening, Revival, and Reformation.
