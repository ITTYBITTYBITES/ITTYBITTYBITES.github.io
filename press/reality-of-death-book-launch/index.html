New Book Confronts the Most Important Question Many Families Avoid

During National Hospice and Palliative Care Month, Dr. Earle S. Gregory challenges readers to prepare not only for the end of life but for eternity.

Gilbert, AZ — November 16, 2026

In recognition of National Hospice + Palliative Care Month — November 2026

Most people make plans for retirement, but how many are prepared for what happens after death? In his new book, The Reality of Death: Are You Ready? (Redemption Press, 2026), educator and ministry leader Dr. Earle S. Gregory confronts one of life’s most avoided questions, offering both practical end-of-life guidance and biblical insight into eternity. Released during National Hospice and Palliative Care Month, the book encourages readers to address important decisions before time runs out.

For those facing retirement, caring for aging loved ones, or quietly questioning their own mortality, Gregory offers a path from uncertainty toward preparation and peace. Drawing on his experience serving as an executor of wills and power of attorney, he addresses wills, financial affairs, end-of-life wishes, and ways to spare families unnecessary burdens. But his central message goes deeper: preparing for death also means examining one’s relationship with God, seeking forgiveness, resolving unfinished business, and finding assurance of eternal life through Jesus Christ.

One encounter helped bring that message into focus. While visiting a ninety-year-old man in rehabilitation, Gregory witnessed a conversation about the man’s end-of-life wishes. When Gregory’s wife subsequently asked where he believed he would go after death, the man’s answer was startling: “Someplace in between.”

For Gregory, that response exposed a troubling reality: People may prepare financially and medically for death while remaining uncertain about eternity. His desire is to help readers move beyond fear and avoidance, putting both their practical affairs and their spiritual lives in order while there is still time.

The Reality of Death: Are You Ready? releases in paperback November 17, 2026. The e-book will be available as a free Amazon download through November 21, 2026.

Dr. Earle S. Gregory is a seasoned educator, author, and ministry leader who came to faith in Christ at age sixty-seven. Now seventy-six, he draws on decades of professional experience, ministry involvement, and biblical study to encourage others to live intentionally, prepare wisely, and face eternity with confidence in Christ.
