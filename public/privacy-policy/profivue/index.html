Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

At Itty Bitty Bites, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your data within the Profivue mobile application.

1. Data Minimization

Profivue is a "local-first" application. This means your sensitive business data stays where it belongs: on your phone.

  • Calculation History: All your buy/sell data is stored in a private database on your device. We never upload this to a server.
  • Marketplace Profiles: Custom fee structures you create are strictly local.

2. Advertising & Analytics

To keep the app free, we use Google AdMob. Google may collect certain identifiers (like your Advertising ID) to serve relevant ads. You can opt-out of personalized ads in your Android system settings.

3. Data Safety Declaration

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data. Because we don't collect your personal info (names, emails, or phone numbers), there is no data for us to share.

4. Third-Party Links

The app contains links to third-party services (like AdMob). We recommend reviewing the Google Privacy Policy for more details on their data handling.
